Private use

Personal Bank Overview is for the owner's personal, non-commercial use with their own eligible ABN AMRO and ING payment accounts. Connect only accounts you own and are authorized to access. The application is not offered to other users.

Read-only access and consent

The application reads booked transactions and balances through Enable Banking after the owner authorizes access. It cannot initiate payments, move money or change bank settings. Bank and Enable Banking terms apply separately. Access may expire or be withdrawn. The owner can disconnect and should verify revocation with the bank.

Using the reports

Reports reflect data available at the last successful synchronization and may be incomplete or delayed. Transfers between the owner's accounts may appear on both sides of a summary. Check figures against official bank records before relying on them. The application is for personal recordkeeping and provides no financial advice.

Protecting data

The owner is responsible for protecting the local Windows profile and application key, checking which accounts are authorized and reviewing reports before sharing them. Third-party storage and AI services have their own terms and privacy practices. See the Privacy Policy.
